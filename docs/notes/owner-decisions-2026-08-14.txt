Q-04   F-3 — are SAMPLED assertions admissible at all?

               question about what the test method is allowed to conclude.

RECOMMENDATION Rule them ADMISSIBLE, but only with a declared settling window,

Q-06   12.3 — latch polarity: default-on, or opt-in?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY YOU        The spec says one thing and the implementation does the opposite.
               One of them should change and it is your call which.

RECOMMENDATION Change the SPEC to opt-in, not the implementation to default-on.

REASONING      12.3 says latching is "default on for coil-shaped signals". The
               generator makes it opt-in per signal. Default-on would put a latch
               on every Bool, and each latch costs a register in a mirror that is
               already tight — the deployed area is 37 registers.
               More importantly, a latch changes what a signal MEANS: a latched
               Bool answers "did this ever happen", an unlatched one answers "is
               this true now". Making that switch by default would silently
               change the meaning of every existing Bool observation.

CAVEAT WORTH   There is a real asymmetry here that argues for care either way:
KNOWING        forgetting `transient` fails LOUD (gate 5 refuses), but forgetting
               `rearmsEachIndex` fails SILENT (you get a one-shot latch that
               compiles, deploys and reads plausibly). Two flags side by side
               whose omissions fail in opposite directions.
